Compenso

The plug-and-play payroll operations layer — it feeds on whatever a business already uses,
runs the money workflow end-to-end, and resells through agencies.

🔌 Universal Intake

  • AI spreadsheet import — drop any Excel/CSV, columns auto-map to pay data MVP
  • Native time clock — web + mobile PWA MVP
  • Integrations: Clockify, Toggl, QuickBooks Time, GHL V2
  • Approval chains: manager → finance sign-off MVP
  • Data flagged for anomalies before it enters a pay run

💰 Pay Engine

  • Every pay type: salary, hourly, per-job, commission, tips, stipends MVP
  • Mixed teams — employees + contractors in one pay run MVP
  • Weekly / biweekly / monthly / off-cycle & bonus runs MVP
  • Deductions, reimbursements, advances & loan tracking
  • Multi-currency with FX locked at run time V2

🏦 Payouts

  • One-click payout batches: Wise, PayPal, Stripe, bank-file export MVP
  • Status tracked to “landed in account”, not “sent”
  • Split payments across multiple accounts
  • Embedded payroll partner (Check / Zeal) → direct deposit + US tax filing without building compliance V3

🛡️ Compliance & Docs

  • Branded auto-payslips (PDF) every run MVP
  • Country packs — statutory calc templates, start with 1–2 markets
  • Contractor doc collection at onboarding: W-9 / W-8BEN
  • Immutable audit trail on every change MVP
  • Clean exports for accountants & filers (Gusto/ADP/QuickBooks formats)

Why this sells when Gusto / ADP / Deel exist
Five differentiators — each one maps to a documented market complaint.
01

Plug-and-play, not rip-and-replace

Feeds on the client’s existing spreadsheets, clocks and tools. Kills the “control gap” — the #1 SMB payroll complaint.

02

White-label distribution

Agencies (incl. the GHL ecosystem) resell it under their brand. Incumbents can’t follow without cannibalizing themselves.

03

AI-native pre-run audit

Errors caught before money moves, not after. Incumbents bolt AI on; this is built around it.

04

Flat, honest pricing

One number, unlimited runs. Direct attack on the base-fee + per-seat + hidden-add-on maze SMBs hate decoding.

05

Compliance-light now, partner-powered later

Start as the ops layer (zero filing risk). Add real tax filing via embedded payroll APIs when upmarket — the enterprise door stays open.

Roadmap — SMB now, enterprise path later
Each phase is independently sellable. No phase depends on winning the previous market first.
Phase 1 · MVP

Payroll Ops for SMBs

Sell to: agencies, dev shops, service SMBs with mixed employee/contractor teams
  • Universal intake (AI import + time clock + approvals)
  • Pay engine: all pay types, mixed teams, off-cycle runs
  • Payout batches + status tracking
  • Payslips, audit trail, accountant exports
  • People portal (payslips + docs)
  • Pre-run AI audit v1
Phase 2 · V2

White-label & Channel

Sell to: agencies as resellers — they bring the SMBs
  • White-label mode (logo/domain/colors)
  • GHL marketplace app
  • Time-tool + accounting integrations
  • AI copilot: NL queries + explain-my-payslip
  • Analytics suite + profitability reports (labor cost vs revenue) + agency margin view
  • Multi-currency runs
Phase 3 · V3

Upmarket & Embedded

Sell to: mid-market + SaaS builders who embed payroll
  • Embedded filing partner (Check/Zeal) → full US payroll
  • Embed API + widgets
  • SSO, SCIM, advanced permissions
  • More country packs
  • SOC 2 track
Business model & honest risks
Flat pricing is itself a feature in this market.

Revenue lines

Direct SaaSFlat tiers by active workers (e.g. $29 / $79 / $199 per month), unlimited pay runs — no per-run or hidden fees
Agency licenseWholesale seats — agency pays a flat platform fee, resells at their own price under their brand
Embed APIUsage-based, per active worker per month (Phase 3)
Filing add-onPass-through margin on the embedded payroll partner (Phase 3)

⚠️ Risks to respect

  • Deel/Remote own global contractor payments — don’t fight them on cross-border rails; track payouts, don’t move money at first
  • GHL marketplace approval is a gate, not a given
  • Embedded filing partners (Check/Zeal) have revenue minimums — Phase 3 needs traction first
  • “Payroll” word sets compliance expectations — market Phase 1 as payroll operations
